The challenge

Cultures Resort is a family-run traditional African restaurant in Hillside, Harare, established in 2018, known for charcoal-grilled meats and traditional stews, and for hosting weddings, roora ceremonies, birthdays and other functions on top of day-to-day dining. Before this site, every order and every catering enquiry went through phone calls and WhatsApp, with no way to manage them in one place.

The brief was to give the restaurant a proper online presence: a way for customers to order and enquire directly, a dedicated space for events and catering, and a back-end the team could actually run the business from — not just a page describing it.

What we built

A site built around the restaurant's real photography, menu and details — the client specifically wanted authentic photos of the actual venue and food, not stock or AI-generated images.

Online ordering
Customers can order directly instead of relying on a phone call
Events & catering
A dedicated page for weddings, roora, birthdays and other functions, hosted on-site or off-site
Admin dashboard
Overview, Orders, Kitchen, Reservations, Enquiries, Menu, Beverages and Events, all in one place
Secure admin login
Authenticated access to the dashboard, so order and enquiry data isn't open to the public
Reservations
A booking flow separate from the events/catering enquiry path
Real venue & food photography
The restaurant's own photos throughout, at the client's request

Result

Cultures Resort now has a single online home for ordering, reservations and catering enquiries — live at culturesresort.co.zw — backed by an admin dashboard the team uses to manage orders, the kitchen queue and enquiries without going back to phone and WhatsApp for everything.

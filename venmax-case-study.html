The challenge

VenMax offers airport transfers, self-drive hire and chauffeur services from a fleet ranging from hybrid sedans to premium 4x4s. Before this site, most of that information — pricing, fleet options, hire terms and how to actually book — lived scattered across social media, making it hard for customers to get a clear picture or reach the team quickly.

The brief was straightforward: a professional site that presents the real fleet and pricing clearly, explains the self-drive terms up front, and gets a customer from "browsing" to a WhatsApp or call enquiry in as few steps as possible.

What we built

A responsive site built around the client's real assets — logo, office and fleet photography, and current pricing — rather than stock content or placeholders.

  • ✓Full fleet listing across self-drive, chauffeur and airport-transfer categories, with daily rates and deposit amounts kept current
  • ✓Clear self-drive hire terms (license, ID, proof of residence, mileage allowance) shown up front to cut down on back-and-forth enquiries
  • ✓Multiple direct contact paths — WhatsApp, phone and email — so a customer can reach VenMax the way they prefer
  • ✓Free airport pickup and free Harare vehicle delivery called out as a differentiator
  • ✓Built and shipped on a lean budget, without unnecessary tooling or ongoing platform costs
